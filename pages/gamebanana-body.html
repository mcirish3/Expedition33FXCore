CPU scheduling and audio fix for Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 on older CPUs

Expedition33FXCore is a small DLL that loads with the game and changes how Windows hands out CPU time to the game's threads. It also gives the engine a bigger audio buffer, which cuts down on the crackling and static. It doesn't touch graphics settings, game files or saves. It is a port of my Windrose and Everwind mods to Expedition 33 (Unreal Engine 5.4).

Measured on my PC

AMD FX-8350, Radeon RX 6600, 1080p, all Epic, XeSS at native resolution, 60 FPS limit. Recorded with PresentMon, with stock and modded scheduling alternated every minute in one session:

  • Average FPS: 22.2 to 25.8 in a light area (+16%), 20.7 to 23.0 in a heavy area (+11%)
  • Stutters over 50 ms: 137 a minute to 40
  • Audio dropouts (crackle): about 25 a minute to about 3 with the bigger audio buffer. The sessions weren't in exactly the same areas, so take the number loosely.

Your results will differ. Newer CPUs have less to gain.

What the mod changes

  • Keeps the frame threads apart. The game, render and RHI threads each prefer their own core or module.
  • Keeps loading work out of the way. Asset streaming and decompression run on the other cores.
  • Protects the audio thread. The audio mixer gets its own preferred core and time-critical priority.
  • Bigger audio buffer (on by default): 2048 frames x 4 instead of the game's 256. Adds up to about 0.17 s of audio delay.
  • Steadier frame pacing. 0.5 ms timer resolution, Above Normal priority for the frame threads, and no Windows power throttling for the game.

Install

  1. In Steam, right-click Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, then Manage, then Browse local files.
  2. Extract the zip into that folder. version.dll and Expedition33FXCore.ini end up in Sandfall\Binaries\Win64.
  3. Start the game. An Expedition33FXCore.log appears next to the DLL and shows your CPU and the threads it adjusted. No log means it didn't load.

Steam version only. Every fix has its own switch in Expedition33FXCore.ini.

Uninstall

Set AudioBufferFix=0 and start the game once, so your Engine.ini is restored. Then delete version.dll, Expedition33FXCore.ini, Expedition33FXCore.log and Expedition33FXCore.state from Sandfall\Binaries\Win64.

Good to know

  • Single-player, no anti-cheat. The DLL makes no network connections.
  • It conflicts with any other mod that also loads through version.dll. It works alongside OptiScaler.
  • On my PC, frame generation smeared badly at 20-25 real FPS, and XeSS native ran better than FSR 4 native.
  • Please post your CPU, GPU and log in the comments.
